Ferrandi

A French pastry class in Paris

Put on an apron and bake a classic French dessert with a pastry chef from FERRANDI Paris, France's most famous culinary school. Then taste what you made.

Where
Ferrandi kitchen, 3rd floor, Lafayette Maison & Gourmet, 35 Boulevard Haussmann, Paris 9e
How long
2 to 3 hours, tasting included
Group
At most 6 people, in French or English
When
Wednesday, Friday or Saturday at 3 PM. You pick the date.

What you might bake

The recipe changes with the season: Paris-Brest, vanilla flan with hazelnut praline, Yule logs at Christmas, tarte Tatin, chocolate tart or mille-feuille.

Arrive 5 to 10 minutes early and bring ID. See the class page
